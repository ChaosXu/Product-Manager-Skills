# 工作坊引导 (Workshop Facilitation) 术语表

| 术语 | 中文 | 解释 | 引用 |
|------|------|------|------|
| Guided | 引导模式 | Entry mode where the facilitator leads the participant through the experience step-by-step, providing structure and direction | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Context dump | 上下文倾倒 | Entry mode where the participant shares all relevant context before the facilitator helps them process and prioritize | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Best guess | 最佳猜测 | Entry mode where the participant makes their best attempt at answering before receiving guidance | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| One-question turn | 单问题轮次 | A turn structure where only one question is asked at a time, allowing focused responses and follow-up | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Progress label | 进度标签 | A label showing where the participant is in the overall workflow (e.g., "Step 2 of 5") | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Interruption | 中断 | When a participant needs to pause or redirect the conversation flow | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Entry mode | 进入模式 | The initial approach for starting an interactive session (Guided, Context dump, or Best guess) | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Turn | 轮次 | A single exchange in the conversation, typically one question from facilitator and one response from participant | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Facilitator | 引导师 | The person guiding the participant through the workshop or interactive skill | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
| Participant | 参与者 | The person receiving guidance or going through the interactive experience | workshop-facilitation/SKILL.md |
